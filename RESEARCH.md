# Belle Pubs & Hotels: research

Compiled 17 October 2026 for a speculative redesign pitch. Everything below was seen in a source listed in section 11; where sources disagree, both are given. Anything not verified is marked **unverified**.

Method: one script pulled all 14 pages in their Wix sitemap. One download script then fetched every image at full size (129 files), all 11 menu PDFs and 47 third-party pages. A second, smaller batch covered review sites. PDF text and fonts were extracted with poppler, and colours were sampled from the PDFs, logos and photos with ImageMagick. Image provenance is in `assets/source/MANIFEST.md`.

DesignMyNight's Hammersmith listing repeats the 2-for-1, Wine-y Thursday and Fizz Friday.

### 1.2 Rosie Galligan: where things stand now

The homepage copy is out of date on several points:

- It says she "featured in the delayed 2021 Rugby World Cup" and that "her aspiration is to be part of the 2025 World Cup playing squad… hopes to reach the final… at Allianz Stadium, Twickenham". **She did, and England won.** She is a 2025 Women's Rugby World Cup winner; Wikipedia's medal table shows gold at "2025 England" and silver in 2021.
- It calls her a "Saracens and England rugby player". **She left Saracens for Harlequins in June 2026** (BBC Sport, "England's Galligan returns to Quins from Saracens", 16 June 2026). Her partner Marlie Packer made the same move (BBC Sport, 10 June 2026).
- Events still current: she cut the ribbon on the Hammersmith Belle garden on **24 May 2025** with Cllr Zarar Qayyum (Enfield Independent / Newsquest; Caterer Licensee). England caps: 31 per the Wikipedia infobox.
- I did not use anything about her personal life. Aggregator sites carry claims I could not confirm with a major outlet.

### 5.3 Type (from the PDFs and posters)

- **Amerigo BT** (Bitstream, Bold and Roman) sets every menu, headings and body: a flared, calligraphic serif. It is a commercial font. The closest free match I tested against a 200 dpi crop of the menu is **Alegreya**, which has the same calligraphic flare, similar caps at weight 700–800 and a full family with italics. Marcellus is too light, Spectral too regular, and Amarante too quirky.
- **Audhistine**: Victorian swash display, used for venue names. I couldn't confirm a web licence, so venue names will use the **logo artwork itself**, not a font.
- **A condensed bold sans**, very close to **Oswald**, on the Fizz Fridays and weekly-offers posters ("HALF PRICE PROSECCO", "2 for 1", "WEEKLY OFFERS"). This is their 'shouting' face for offers.
- **Baskerville SemiBold** appears in two PDFs.
- **Menu layout habits**: headings flanked by thin rules ("——— STARTERS ———"); thin **double-rule frames with notched art-deco corners** around the page; boxed call-outs ("BIG BELLE £24.95", "HOMEMADE CRISPY ONION RINGS Simply the best!"); item names in small caps in Belle blue, followed by the description in ink.

## 6. What guests say

Ratings are snapshots from listing pages or search results on 17 October 2026. TripAdvisor, Google and Booking.com pages blocked direct fetching; see section 10.

**Praised, consistently**

- **Staff**: friendly, helpful, "not large-hotel drones" (Belle's own quoted review). This comes up at every venue and on Tagvenue for Moretown.
- **Breakfast** included with rooms: Fitzrovia breakfast scores 9/10 on Kayak, and it is the most consistent praise for the Griffin.
- **Location**: Fitzrovia 10/10 on Kayak; the Griffin for the US Embassy ("16 min walk"), the Oval and Vauxhall; the Duchess "across from the Power Station and the tube".
- **Clean, comfortable beds and good showers**: Kayak tags "great shower", "clean and comfy".
- **The flowers**: the Duchess's "walls of selfie-ready flowers and beautiful chandeliers"; "the outdoor flowers are in abundance and so pretty".
- **Big-game atmosphere** at Moretown (Tagvenue). The Moretown Christmas carvery gets a mention.
- **Hammersmith**: "a very inviting garden"; "took advantage of the 2 for 1 burgers and they were just fabulous", plus the sweet potato fries (TripAdvisor, Sept 2026).

**Complaints, consistently**

- **Noise**: from street-facing rooms, the pub below, signage and next door (Griffin, Fitzrovia).
- **Heat and no air-conditioning** (Duchess, Griffin, Fitzrovia). One Duchess room has no window, and a guest said Booking.com didn't make that clear.
- **Small rooms** (Griffin attic rooms, Duchess "compact"), **no lifts**, patchy Wi-Fi.
- **Moretown deposits**: non-refundable and only usable as store credit (TripAdvisor). There are also isolated, unverified serious complaints (2023, 2025) that I have not repeated.
- **Duchess regular**: "the decor used to be great & unique. Replaced by flowers & so many TVs."
- **Hammersmith neighbours**: Wooden Spoon (section 2.5).

## 12. Design direction (each choice traced to the evidence above)

**Idea: "The menu card meets match night."** Their own line has two halves, *Beautiful* + *Sports Pubs & Hotel Rooms*, and so do the pubs: rose walls and powder-blue menus on one side, walls of screens and neon on the other. The site has two matching registers:

- **Day / stay / eat**: powder blue and blush paper, rose clusters, thin double-rule frames, Alegreya (§5.2–5.4).
- **Match night / fixtures**: the chequerboard floor, near-black, neon rose and Oswald scoreboard type (§5.3 posters, §5.4 neon and chequer).

**Palette**: tokens from §5.2. Body text uses a deepened Belle blue (`#2D5F7C`, derived from `#9DCBE4` for 4.5:1 contrast) and menu ink `#373C41`. The CTA is rose crimson `#D22D4E`; match-night accents are the same crimson pushed to a neon glow. Offer tags are butter `#EED8A0`, and veg/vegan markers use leaf `#2D6B2B`.

**Type**: Alegreya, the closest free match to the Amerigo BT on every menu (§5.3). **Oswald** for fixtures, kick-off times, prices and offers, matching their poster face. **Venue names use the real logo artwork**, converted from the CMYK sources, with Moretown in its script and the others in swash capitals. No other fonts.

**Motifs**:

- Hand-drawn SVG rose sprays (pink, crimson, peach and the single yellow rose) tumbling from corners, as on the menus.
- Notched double-rule frames on cards and menus.
- "——— Heading ———" rules.
- Chequerboard bands for match-night sections.
- A flower-canopy edge on photo heroes.
- Neon-glow type for the Belle Burgers moment.
- A drawn map of London's river with the five Belles pinned.

**Venue pages, one idea each** (§2):

- **Moretown**: a "wall of screens" hero, a grid of live tiles running fixtures, and the circular bar.
- **Duchess**: flowers and a Power Station skyline drawing, the 1789/1883 history, and the balcony.
- **Fitzrovia**: the black-and-gold shopfront as a sign-painted header, and the five rooms up front.
- **Griffin**: the heraldic griffin and Vauxhall Motors story, 14 rooms, the Haunted Hotel night and the 2020 award.
- **Hammersmith**: the Enchanted Garden with a drawn garden plan (beach huts, castle, pergola), the darts lounge, the Kids' Table and the 1883 facade.
